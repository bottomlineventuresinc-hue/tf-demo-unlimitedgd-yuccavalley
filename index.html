What we will not do

  • Quote a door or a gate from photos alone.
  • Hide parts costs inside a vague line item.
  • Start before the scope, including what happens to the old door or gate, is agreed.
  • Leave a door or gate without checking that it opens and closes properly.
  • Leave old parts or packaging behind.
